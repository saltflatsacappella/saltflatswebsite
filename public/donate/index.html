Donate

The Salt Flats A Cappella Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations go straight to support the group and our mission to share great vocal music in the university space. Your donations are what keep the group going: sheet music and arrangements, getting everyone to the ICCAs, and renting the space and gear for our concerts.
