Hi, we're The Salt Flats

We're the University of Utah's best (and only) competitive a cappella team. We rehearse a few nights a week, compete in the ICCAs every year, and put on two big concerts on campus, one in the winter and one in the spring. In between, you'll find us at campus events, weddings, and holiday parties all over the Salt Lake valley.

Got an event? We'd love to sing at it.
